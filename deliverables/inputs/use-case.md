Hackathon Challenge: Meeting Minutes Generator

Background: Business Analysts spend significant time manually reviewing meeting transcripts and preparing Minutes of Meeting (MoM). This process is time-consuming and may result in missed decisions, action items, or important discussion points.

Challenge: Build a tool that converts raw meeting transcripts into a structured, downloadable Minutes of Meeting (MoM) document.

Expected Outcome: The generated MoM should clearly capture key discussion points, decisions made, action items, responsible owners, and deadlines (if mentioned).
